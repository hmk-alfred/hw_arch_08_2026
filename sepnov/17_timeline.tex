\section{September--November Timeline}
\label{sec:timeline}

Current date: September~9, 2026. Final deadline: November~30, 2026. The cadence of the prior plan is preserved; the deliverables are revised for the corrected LAN8660/LAN8661/LAN8651 role assignment (Section~\ref{sec:hardware-roles}).
