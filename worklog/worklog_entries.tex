\worklogentry{2026-09-09}{Revision 2 of sepnov\_plan.tex: LAN8660/LAN8661 promoted to mandatory Greenfield endpoints, common-node premise retired}{
The user revised the Sept--Nov plan's hardware architecture based directly on
the LAN8660/LAN8661 finding from the 2026-09-08 session and a follow-up
discussion about whether those parts could be shared across the Greenfield
and Brownfield architectures. Conclusion reached in discussion and confirmed
by the user's new instructions: they cannot be shared as silicon (RCP is not
a general transport an MCU can drive arbitrary payloads through, and their
I\textsuperscript{2}C/SPI/UART pins face outward to peripherals, not inward
to a host controller), so the prior plan's ``one MCU+LAN8651 PCB becomes
either E2B or Gateway by population'' premise is retired entirely. New
architecture: LAN8660 (Control Endpoint) and LAN8661 (Lighting Endpoint) are

secondary/time-boxed investigation as Revision~1 treated them -- used
MCU-less and RCP-managed, with zero Alfred firmware at the edge. LAN8651

Clicker~4, and the Brownfield Gateway MCU). The three node classes (E2B-
Control/LAN8660, E2B-Lighting/LAN8661, Gateway/MCU+LAN8651) are now
