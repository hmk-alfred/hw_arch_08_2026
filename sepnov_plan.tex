\textit{This revision supersedes Revision~1 of this document (2026-09-08). It corrects the hardware-role assignment for LAN8660 and LAN8661: both are now mandatory, first-class Greenfield E2B endpoints, not a secondary/time-boxed investigation. Scope: 10BASE-T1S architecture, LAN8651 host-side integration, LAN8660 Control Endpoint validation, LAN8661 Lighting Endpoint validation, PLCA, E2B electrical architecture, brownfield vehicle Gateway architecture, CAN/CAN-FD, LIN if schedule permits, electrical protection, PCB development, network characterization, quantitative validation, and customer demonstration hardware -- from September~8, 2026 through the November~30, 2026 exit gate. Central-compute selection, zonal-compute architecture, firmware generation, SIL/HIL, ML/application software architecture, autonomous driving, infotainment, and production ISO~26262/ISO-SAE~21434/EMC/environmental qualification are explicitly out of scope.
